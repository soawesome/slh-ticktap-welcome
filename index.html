Email confirmed. Welcome to SLH TickTap.

Your account is ready. Here is how to start.

  1. Open SLH TickTap on your iPhoneInstall it from TestFlight if you have not yet. You need the TestFlight invitation from the team.
  2. Turn on a passcodeThe app will not scan without one. Settings > Face ID & Passcode (or Touch ID & Passcode).
  3. Sign inUse the same email and the password you chose when you signed up.
  4. Scan a wristband to clock inPoint the camera at the wristband QR code. When the patient is done, scan again to clock out and pick the bill time.

Good to know

  • The app never stores a patient's NRIC in readable form.
  • If you are offline, your scans are kept on the phone and sent when you are back online.
  • Forgot your password? Ask the TickTap team to reset it.
  • This is a pilot. Please use test wristbands only until the team says otherwise.
